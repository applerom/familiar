T: Rosa held the small sleeve against the daylight and picked out a loose thread. Across the roofs a mill whistle told her Amaro would be home before supper. Their daughter Helena was already asleep, with both fists closed.
R: record=doc-helena-baptism | cut=Rosa Calhau, nat. de Ponta d[...] | reading=The invented baptism paper names Rosa as Helena's mother. The torn edge takes the rest of the place name.
T: Rosa set the finished sleeve across the back of a chair. When the baby woke, she took her to the window to watch for Amaro.

KIDS: Rosa mended Helena's sleeve. Then they watched for Amaro to come home from the mill.
