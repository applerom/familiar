# Polish record-reading answers

## RP-60

- Historical `-ówna/-anka/-owa` and `wdowa po …` must be read as a whole formula in the record; a suffix alone does not identify a father, husband or generation. Basis: ex-ussr-kinship-research v1.44.0, references/field-lessons.md:139, outside observation, not rechecked by Familiar. [reported]

## RP-66

- `z …` after a mother's name can introduce her maiden surname; distinguish a place through grammar, the column and parallel acts. Basis: ex-ussr-kinship-research v1.44.0, references/field-lessons.md:139, outside observation, not rechecked by Familiar. [reported]
- Read the complete surname and marital-state formula before assigning a relationship; the ending is a reading cue only. Basis: the same outside observation at references/field-lessons.md:139. [reported]
